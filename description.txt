Engine Optimizer - diagnostics and engine-level patches for tModLoader.

Hotkeys: F6 - overlay, F7 - emergency hardware-cursor toggle, F8 - world
preload on/off (stops the running preload immediately, or starts it from
where the player stands).

Frame profiling: draw-to-draw FPS, frame time, update/draw split, present/other,
lighting, tiles, walls, background, dust, projectiles, NPC and item draw time,
per-section update timings, GC activity, allocation rate, entity counts.

On-disk diagnostics: live.json (1 s snapshot, incl. per-type NPC.AI cost and
how much update time is outside the known sections), timeline.csv (5 s rows),
spikes.csv (every frame over 25 ms with its dominant cause: preload / save /
GC / section / GPU / update / draw) and last-session.txt on world exit: boss
vs calm FPS, spike causes, projectile/NPC tops, worst 20 frames and automatic
recommendations (vsync, lighting, GPU-bound, GC, frame skip).

Smoothness: SustainedLowLatency GC, whole-world tile-frame preload on entry
(re-centers on the player after teleports, adaptive per-frame budget, works in
single player and as a multiplayer client), autosave postponed during boss
fights, asynchronous telemetry I/O, cached overlay.

Parallelism: vanilla gore on the thread pool (visual only), telemetry on a
background thread. Rain parallelism is experimental and off by default.
Gameplay simulation (NPC / projectiles / tiles / liquids) is never
parallelized - it is unsafe in Terraria.

Also fills in missing mod keybind profile entries so mods that read their
keybind on world entry cannot crash the game when it boots straight into a
world. The mod does not touch the mouse cursor at all (the cursor subsystem
was removed in 1.3 after it caused flicker/disappearing cursors on some
setups; if your cursor disappears, that is another mod - BossCursor etc.).
